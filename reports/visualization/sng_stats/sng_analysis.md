# sng_parser Prompt Complexity Analysis

All datasets are parsed from prompt text with `sng_parser`. Attributes are parser modifiers, entities are parser entities, and relations are parser relations.

## Ours

- Prompts: 3099
- Avg attributes/entity: 1.23
- Entities with attributes: 82.07%
- Avg relations/object: 0.52
- Objects/prompt: 5.39
- Relations/prompt: 2.81

## Comparison Notes

- Ours has higher attributes/entity than 7 of 9 comparison datasets.
- Ours has higher percent entities with attributes than 5 of 9 comparison datasets.
- Ours has higher relations/object than 7 of 9 comparison datasets.

See `sng_summary.csv`, `sng_per_prompt_distributions.csv`, and the PNG plots in this directory.
